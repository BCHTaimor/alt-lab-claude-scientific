# Hypothesis Development

Use this skill to develop ideas grounded in known biology and to prevent a plausible narrative from being mistaken for a supported mechanism.

## Workflow

1. Restate the underlying mechanistic question.
2. Identify relevant biology, methods, and prior observations.
3. Propose a working model and credible alternative models.
4. Derive distinct predictions from each model.
5. Suggest experiments or observations that discriminate among the models.
