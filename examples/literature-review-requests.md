# Literature Review Requests

## Input

"For a lab-meeting discussion, summarize the leading models for how RAG scanning is constrained at the Igk locus, emphasizing primary papers from the past ten years and unresolved mechanistic questions."

## Expected Behavior

Route the request directly to Literature Review. Select literature search and triage, citation and evidence, literature synthesis, and response patterns; use other skills only when the task requires them.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The answer distinguishes established findings from unresolved questions.
- Citations are real and verified.
- The response explains why the selected literature is relevant to the lab-meeting purpose.
