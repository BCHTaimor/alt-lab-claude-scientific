# Example Requests and Analysis Targets

## Example Requests This Document Is Meant To Support

- "Find the most important papers on locus contraction during V(D)J recombination."
- "What are the main models for how ATM suppresses aberrant joining in developing B cells?"
- "Can you help me think through why we might see this phenotype after knocking out factor X?"
- "Explain the difference between classical NHEJ and alternative end joining in this context."
- "Give me a reading list for someone joining the lab and trying to understand B-cell recombination."
- "What are the biggest unresolved questions in RAG off-target activity?"
- "Analyze this older paper and explain what it established versus what later work revised."
- "Walk me through the logic of the experiments in `Molecular basis for differential Igk versus Igh V(D)J joining mechanisms`."
- "Help me understand what `Linear RAG scanning mediates editing of Igk variable region repertoires` changed about the field's model."

## Example Analysis Targets

Claude Scientific should work well on papers like:

- `Molecular basis for differential Igk versus Igh V(D)J joining mechanisms`
- `Linear RAG scanning mediates editing of Igk variable region repertoires`

For papers like these, useful output includes:

- a short statement of the paper's main mechanistic claim
- the 2-4 experiments that carry most of the argument
- the assumptions or limitations of those experiments
- how the paper connects to broader questions in B-cell development and antigen receptor locus biology
- whether later papers strengthened, refined, or challenged the model
